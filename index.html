What I build

Four things. Built to work together.

Most businesses need one or two of these. They work best when they share the same data.

AI agents

WhatsApp and web chat agents that answer from your real prices, stock and calendar. They quote, book and hand over to a person when it matters.

e.g. Alec, RISKPROP Realty

Business websites

Fast sites on your own domain, with WhatsApp, call and quote buttons on every screen, and online payments when you need them.

e.g. jackenterprise.co.za

Systems and databases

One database for your stock, jobs or clients, with a simple screen your team uses every day. Everything else reads from it.

e.g. the Stock Manager

Automations

Reminders, follow ups, reports and alerts that run on their own and connect the tools you already use.

e.g. ROY, utility bill checks
